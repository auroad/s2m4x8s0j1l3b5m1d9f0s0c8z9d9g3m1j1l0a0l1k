Protect Your Coinbase Account

Learn how to stay safe from unauthorized transactions

🔐 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Always enable 2FA using apps like Google Authenticator or Authy to add an extra layer of security.

📧 Use a Strong Email Password

Your email is the gateway to your account. Make sure it has a strong, unique password and 2FA enabled.

🚫 Beware of Phishing Scams

Never click suspicious links or share your login details. Always verify emails and websites before entering credentials.

💻 Secure Your Device

Keep your phone and computer updated with antivirus software and avoid using public Wi-Fi for transactions.

🔎 Monitor Account Activity

Regularly check your account for any unusual activity and enable notifications for logins and transactions.

🛑 What To Do If You Notice Unauthorized Activity

  • Immediately change your password
  • Disable withdrawals if possible
  • Contact Coinbase support
  • Check linked bank accounts

Stay Alert, Stay Secure

Your security is in your hands. Take action today to protect your crypto assets.
